## Overview

{{ entity_label_plural_title }} on a paid plan manage subscription status, payment methods, and invoices through **Subscriptions** in the **System Support** section of the {{ entity_label }} menu.

## Steps

1. Switch to the {{ entity_label }} you administer (if you belong to more than one).
2. Open **Subscriptions** from the {{ entity_label }} menu.
3. Review the current plan, trial status, and next billing date.
4. To change plans or update a card, use **Manage billing** when you have **Manage Billing** permission.
5. Download invoices or receipts from the billing portal when Stripe provides them.

Members with **View Billing** or **View Subscription Status** can review status but cannot change plans unless they also hold manage permission.

## Trials and entitlements

During a trial, some packages (such as Voting) may remain available until the trial ends or the subscription becomes active. If a feature is locked, check subscription status first.

When a subscription lapses, the dashboard may show a **Subscription required** banner and some routes become inaccessible until billing is restored.
